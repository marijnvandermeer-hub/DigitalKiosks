# Digital Kiosks

This repository contains research data, analytical code, methodological documentation, and reproducibility materials developed within the **Digital Kiosks** project.

Digital Kiosks explores neighbourhood-based solutions that support product sharing and more circular patterns of consumption. The research materials in this repository support different activities within the project and are organised by activity.

---

## Research activities

Research materials are organised in the `research/` directory. Each activity has its own folder containing the relevant documentation, analytical scripts, data where publicly available, and analytical outputs.

### Activity 1.4 – A database on individual ownership

**Household product ownership, use, and sharing potential across six European cities**

Activity 1.4 investigates the ownership and use of selected household products and their potential relevance for neighbourhood sharing stations.

The study uses household survey data from six participating European cities to examine product ownership, underutilisation, demographic differences, awareness and use of circular economy initiatives, willingness to borrow products, and motivations for and barriers to using sharing stations.

**[View Activity 1.4 research materials →](research/activity-1.4-individual-ownership/)**

Additional research activities and supporting materials will be added as they become available.

## Data and code

Where possible, this repository provides the materials required to understand and reproduce the analyses conducted within the Digital Kiosks project, including:

- analytical scripts;
- methodological documentation;
- variable definitions and codebooks;
- analysis-ready data, where these can be made publicly available;
- generated tables and figures; and
- reproducibility and verification documentation.

The exact materials available may differ between research activities.

## Data protection

Research data are only made publicly available where this is consistent with informed-consent conditions, project data-management arrangements, institutional requirements, and applicable data-protection legislation.

Where respondent-level or otherwise sensitive data cannot be publicly distributed, the repository may still provide analytical code, methodological documentation, variable definitions, generated outputs, and information on applicable data-access conditions.
